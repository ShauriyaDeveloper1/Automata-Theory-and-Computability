How String Simulation Works (The Non-Tech Intuition)
1. NFA: The "Team"

When reading a letter, the NFA can explore multiple arrows at once. It splits itself like a team. At the end, if at least one member of the team is on an accepting state, the word is ACCEPTED!

2. DFA: The "Single Explorer"

The DFA only ever has ONE active state. For every letter, it follows exactly one guaranteed arrow. At the end, if that single state is accepting, the word is ACCEPTED!

3. Perfect Agreement

Even though the NFA branches into groups and the DFA takes a single path, the Subset Construction guarantees they always agree on the final Accepted or Rejected verdict!
